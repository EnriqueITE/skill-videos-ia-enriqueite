The workflow

From script to final cut.

Research

Define your audience and topic. Check each claim against its original source and choose your angle before writing.

Approve the script

Review the hook, timing and narration. The workflow pauses for you to approve the text before preparing the scenes.

Render locally

Gather images, narration and subtitles. The Python script reads escenas.json and uses FFmpeg to export a portrait or landscape MP4.

https://github.com/EnriqueITE/skill-videos-ia-enriqueite
Use the videos-ia-enriqueite folder containing SKILL.md.

Local rendering requires Python 3.10 or later, Pillow, NumPy and FFmpeg with libx264. You can record your own narration.
